Bethel Samji Gurukkal

Samji Gurukkal (Founder in richard stando gurukulam )
Navajeevanam kalari training Head and codinator

Bethel Samji Gurukkal began his Kalari journey at the age of six and has devoted more than 35 years to the art. He trained under his own Gurukkal, Richard Stando Gurukkal, whose teaching shaped his understanding of Kalaripayattu as both a martial discipline and a way of life.

As the Kalari Training Head and Coordinator at Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam, Samji Gurukkal oversees comprehensive student instruction across Meythari (body conditioning & flexibility), Kolthari (wooden weapons mastery), Angathari (metal weapons & shield combat), and Verumkai (unarmed self-defense and vital pressure-point locks). His profound pedagogical experience ensures that the time-honoured values of discipline, mental focus, humility, and physical mastery are imparted to every student in strict adherence to traditional Gurukulam pedagogy.

Under his steadfast supervision, training routines follow safe, progressive stages that build core vitality and inner composure alongside martial readiness, cultivating individuals who revere their heritage and embody true warrior virtues.

The Four Pillars of Navajeevanam

1. Unaltered Thaliyola Wisdom

Every treatment and oil concoction adheres strictly to ancient palm leaf texts passed down generation to generation.

2. Traditional In-House Preparations

Prepared directly at our pharmacy without artificial perfumes or mass-market diluents. All oils and herbal bundles are prepared on premises.

3. Medicinal Wood Dronis

Treatments take place on traditional wooden beds carved from therapeutic trees that enhance herbal absorption.

4. Transparent Prognosis

We only accept patients where our therapies offer genuine restorative benefit, providing clear timelines from day one.
