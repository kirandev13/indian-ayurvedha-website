The Founder's Journey

Sajith Kalariyil

Ayurveda Kalari Marma Therapy Consultant & Yoga Instructor
Founder of Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam & NORT (Navajeevanam Orthopedic Remedic Therapy Institute)

Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam was established in 2012 as an undertaking of the Indian School of Martial Arts (I.S.M.A) to keep Kerala's sacred warrior-medicine intact and accessible to patients worldwide.

After mastering Kalaripayattu, Sajith Kalariyil continued his intensive studies for 12 unbroken years under legendary Kalari Chikilsa vaidya, Guru Dharmanandha Swaroopa Hanumandas Swamiji. Under Guruji's strict tutelage, he learned the sacred secrets of the 107 Marma pressure points, bone alignment methods, and the confidential formulation of medicinal oils derived from ancient palm leaf manuscripts (thaliyola).

At Navajeevanam, we decide on treatments only after comprehensive physical examination and understanding the individual patient's body constitution. The medicines we use are handmade in-house per thaliyola instructions, and every treatment bed is custom-crafted from medicinal woods such as Venga to amplify healing.

"We provide an honest clinical assessment and dedicated traditional care because our remedies address the actual root cause of neuro-muscular tension rather than merely masking discomfort."

Dedicated Women's Healing Wing

Srilakshmi M

General Manager & Senior Kalari Marma Treatment Specialist
Head of Women's Chikilsa & Clinical Care Operations

At Navajeevanam, we believe authentic traditional healing requires complete personal trust, comfort, and absolute dignity. Our clinic features an exclusively female-led treatment division supervised and administered by Srilakshmi M.

Recognizing that many women hesitate to seek traditional Kalari therapies and herbal oil treatments when only male practitioners are present, Srilakshmi M personally heads all Women's Chikilsa procedures. This ensures that women, working professionals, mothers, young girls, and senior citizens can walk into our clinic freely and receive treatments with complete peace of mind and utmost privacy.

As General Manager and an experienced Kalari Marma Treatment Specialist, she combines deep practical mastery of vital Marma pressure points, therapeutic oil applications, and gentle skeletal alignment with deeply compassionate patient care. Her specialized treatments focus on chronic lower backache, cervical spondylitis, knee arthritis, postpartum musculoskeletal restoration, and stress relief.

"Every woman deserves a healing sanctuary where her health, comfort, and privacy are respected without compromise. Our dedicated women's care ensures every patient can walk in freely and experience hereditary healing."

Srilakshmi M — General Manager & Marma Specialist

The Four Pillars of Navajeevanam

1. Unaltered Thaliyola Wisdom

Every treatment and oil concoction adheres strictly to ancient palm leaf texts passed down generation to generation.

2. Traditional In-House Preparations

Prepared directly at our pharmacy without artificial perfumes or mass-market diluents. All oils and herbal bundles are prepared on premises.

3. Medicinal Wood Dronis

Treatments take place on traditional wooden beds carved from therapeutic trees that enhance herbal absorption.

4. Transparent Prognosis

We only accept patients where our therapies offer genuine restorative benefit, providing clear timelines from day one.
