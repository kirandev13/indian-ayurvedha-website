It applies specialised knowledge of the 107 vital Marma junctures and freshly prepared herbal oils to soothe muscular tension, relieve inflammation, and support healthy mobility through non-invasive traditional techniques.

In many instances of cervical and lumbar spondylitis, back stiffness, and sciatic discomfort, our focused Marma mobilisation, Greeva Vasti (warm oil pooling), and Uzhichil help soothe contracted musculature, reduce nerve tension, and support spinal flexibility. We review medical reports and prior scans during the initial consultation to give an honest evaluation of treatment suitability.

Treatment duration is determined after pulse diagnosis, physical examination, and assessment of your condition's chronicity. Typical courses range from 3 days (introductory diagnosis), 7 days (acute discomfort relief), to 14 or 21 days for long-standing joint or spinal stiffness.
