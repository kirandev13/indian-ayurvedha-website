Traditional Kalari Chikitsa & Marma Healing

Evolved from ancient Ayurveda and Siddha systems to provide rapid, natural recovery from spine degeneration, joint trauma, and chronic musculoskeletal pain.

What is Kalari Chikitsa?

It is a form of treatment which evolved from Ayurveda and was later perfected to meet the demanding need for rapid recovery from combat and sports injuries among the ancient warriors of Kerala. Kalari Chikitsa uses proprietary, secret herbal oil mixes traditionally inherited through palm-leaf manuscripts.

Kalari chikitsa covers all classical Ayurveda and Siddha therapies, but the medicines and diagnostic pressure points used are fundamentally distinct and sacred. We tailor every intervention to the specific body constitution and pathology of the patient.

Kalari Uzhichil

The Sacred Art of Therapeutic Kalari Massage

Uzhichil is a unique hereditary Kalari massage therapy used to rejuvenate the nervous and muscular systems of the body and to stimulate blood circulation. Medicinal oils boiled in our facility are generously applied to the body.

A precise amount of pressure is applied onto the muscles and nadis to invigorate sluggish circulatory and nervous pathways. Uniquely, both the hands and feet of the therapist are employed in specialised continuous strokes.

The 3 Classical Types of Uzhichil:

  • Sukhathirummu: Relaxing, stress-relieving therapy for bodily comfort and vitality.
  • Rakshathirummu: Therapeutic massage traditionally used for musculoskeletal discomfort, postural stiffness, and body ache management.
  • Kachathirummu: Intensive conditioning to improve physical flexibility and joint agility.

Medicated Herbal Poultice

Marma Kizhi (Pindaswedam)

Therapeutic Bundle Massage with Medicated Warmth

A Kizhi is a tightly bound herbal cloth bundle containing traditional medicinal leaves, roots, and herbal powders. The massage given using this medicated bundle is called Kizhithirummu or Pindaswedam.

During the session, the Kizhi is repeatedly dipped into warm medicinal oils (Thailams) formulated for specific inflammation. Owing to the combined effect of active botanical decoctions, traditional herbs, and penetrating warmth, Marma Kizhi is traditionally applied to soothe osteoarthritis discomfort, rheumatic joint pain, muscular fatigue, and localised joint swelling.

Marma Healing

The 107 Vital Prana Junctions of the Body

Marmas are the vital energy intersections in the body close to the skin surface where important nerves form junctures with muscle fibres, veins, bones, and joints. Prana (life energy) flows channelled through these 107 vital points.

When trauma, improper posture, or physical strain creates localised blockages, muscular discomfort and joint stiffness result. Our Vaidyar gently applies calibrated pressure to these points using traditional techniques, helping relieve muscular tightness, improve circulation, and support balanced mobility.

Traditional Care for Cervical & Lumbar Discomfort

Steam & Metabolism

Swedana (Aavikuli) & Nikarsa

Herbal Medicated Steam Bath & Weight Management

Swedana is a traditional steam treatment derived from the Sanskrit word swid ('to sweat'). Generated from boiling medicated herbal decoctions, Kalari steam therapy dilates body channels to promote perspiration, purge metabolic wastes (Ama), and ease body heaviness.

Combined with Nikarsa (Udwarthanam dry herbal powder scrub massage), this protocol safely stimulates skin circulation, tones bodily tissues, and complements an active, disciplined lifestyle.
