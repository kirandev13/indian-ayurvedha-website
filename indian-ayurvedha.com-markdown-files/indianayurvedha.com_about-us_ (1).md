# Navajeevanam Kalari Chikitsa

Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam was established in 2012 and is a well-known Kalari treatment center in Trivandrum. We specialize in hereditary Kalari treatments for spondylitis, joint pain, back pain, knee pain, and weight loss. Our treatments are strictly and honestly based on thaliyola( ancient palm leaf documents)which had been passed down to our Guruji from his ancestors. Hereditary Kalari treatments are entirely different from modern allopathic treatments. We decide on the treatments only after considering the health and body type of the patients. So a patient cannot change the treatment plans as per his/her wish. The medicines we use are handmade as per thaliyola and the furniture used for treatments is made from medicinal wood. We guarantee 100% recovery from the specified illness within the mentioned period. We also offer training programs and packages.

###### About Founder of Navajeevanam

Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam treatment center is one of the best hereditary Kalari treatment centers in Trivandrum. The Sajith Kalariyil (Ayurveda Kalari Marma Therapy Consultant & Yoga Instructor) Founder of Navajeevanam Kalari Vaidyamadam and NORT(Navajeevanam Orthopedic Remedic Therapy Institute) is an energetic & young Kalari vaidya in Trivandrum.

After attaining his Martial Arts in Kalari Payattu, he continues his learning for 12 years under the legendary Kalari Chikilsa vaidya, Guru Dharmanandha Swaroopa Hanumandas Swamiji. He is the guru who has devoted his entire life to Kalari chikilsa and encouraged sajith kalariyil to go forward sticking to the ancient method of treatment followed in Kalari chikilsa. The advice of guru Dharmanandha Swaroopa Hanumandas Swamiji still guides sajith kalariyil in his profession. Navajeevanam Kalari vaidyamadam assures 100% cure in back pain, knee pain, joint pain, spondylitis, and weight loss by using traditional as well as modern approaches. Sajith Kalariyil is specialized in all types of hereditary Kalari as well as ayurvedic treatments.
