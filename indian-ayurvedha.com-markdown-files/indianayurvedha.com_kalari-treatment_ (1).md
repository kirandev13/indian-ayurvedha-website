## KALARI CHIKITSA

     It is a form of treatment which evolved from Ayurveda and was later improved as per the needs of fast recovery from sports injuries for the warriors of ancient Kerala. The Kalari Chikitsa uses different types of oil and other organic content medicines made  out of precious-secret mixes that has been traditionally inherited. So kalari chikitsa is considered to be more effective than Ayurveda by many. Kalari chikitsa covers all ayurveda & siddha treatments, but the medicine used are entirely different and sacred.

### KALARI UZHICHIL

Uzhichil is a unique hereditary kalari massage theraphy used to rejuvenate the nervous and muscular systems of the body and to stimulate blood circulation. Medicinal Oils are used for performing Uzhichil (Kalari massage). A specific amount of pressure is to be applied on to the muscles for stimulating the functioning of the nervous and circulatory systems. Both the hands and feet of the theraphist are used in Uzhichil treatment. There are 3 types of Uzhichil in kalari and they are Sukhathirummu, Rakshathirummu and Kachathirummu.

### MARMA KIZHI

The Kizhi is dipped into medicinal oils (Thilam) during the kalari massage. Where there is pain and swelling, with the right kind of Thailam. Owing to the combined effect of the medicated Kizhi and the warmth, it is highly effective in treating Osteo and Rheumatic (Rheumatoid) arthritis. ### SWEDANA (AAVIKULI)

Swedana is a steam treatment explained in Ayurvedic medical science. The word Sweda is derived from Sanskrit swid, meaning ‘to sweat or to perspire’. So swedana is the process of inducing sweat with the help of steam, generated from medicated herbal decoctions. Due to the secret medicines used, Swedana in kalari is entirely different from Ayurveda. Swedana  can be done on the entire body or on specific body parts. It is a very effective treatment for body swelling and pain.
