Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent information regarding treatment procedures, expected outcomes, and hereditary Kalari healing protocols.

Kalari Chikitsa evolved within Kerala's martial arts heritage to treat physical trauma, sprains, musculoskeletal misalignments, and joint dislocations. Unlike symptomatic pain relief, Kalari therapies work deeply on the body's 107 vital Marma points, combined with warm medicated oils and specialized manipulation techniques, to support natural tissue healing and restore functional mobility.

Yes. We do not use commercial bulk oils. Our medicinal oils (thailams), herbal decoctions (kashayams), and poultice bundles (kizhis) are prepared directly inside our clinic pharmacy following hereditary palm-leaf manuscripts (thaliyola) inherited from our lineage.

Many patients suffering from Cervical Spondylosis, Lumbar Disc Prolapse (L4-L5/L5-S1), and Sciatica experience significant symptom reduction through conservative Kalari care. Our treatments combine warm medicated oil pooling (Greeva/Kati Vasti), tailored Kalari Uzhichil, and careful Marma stimulation to reduce inflammation, release deep muscular tension around compressed nerve roots, and improve spinal flexibility. Case suitability is determined through clinical evaluation and review of existing MRI/X-ray reports.

Kalari Chikitsa is a structured clinical discipline. Our Vaidyar determines the specific therapeutic protocol only after evaluating spinal alignment, joint range of motion, marma sensitivity, and overall constitution. Because therapies work in precise physiological stages, consistency with the recommended plan is critical for therapeutic benefit.
