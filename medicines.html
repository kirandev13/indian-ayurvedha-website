Boiled fresh on premises in sacred copper Urulis according to secret palm-leaf recipes passed down through centuries.

Authentic Botanical Formulations

Hereditary Kalari Thailams & Classical Preparations

The therapeutic foundation of traditional Kalari Chikitsa lies in the purity, sourcing, and patient-specific preparation of its botanical formulations.

Rather than relying on generic bulk products, Navajeevanam prepares its primary thailams, kuzhambus, and kizhi boluses in-house following classical lineages. Every formulation is compounded on clinic premises in controlled batches.

Our thailams (medicinal oils), kuzhambus, churnams (herbal powders), and kizhi boluses are prepared following formulations inscribed on hereditary palm-leaf manuscripts (thaliyola). Each formula combines selected medicinal plants, roots, barks, and cold-pressed sesame oil, simmered over controlled wood flame in heavy vessels until the herbal essences are thoroughly infused into the oil medium.

Authentic Methodology

From Forest Botanical to Healing Application

1

Western Ghats Botanical Sourcing

Wild herbs, fresh rhizomes, and therapeutic tree barks handpicked from organic reserves across Kerala's bio-diverse hills. Harvested during specific lunar phases per traditional pharmacopoeia.

2

Slow Woodfire Simmering in Urulis

Decoctions (Kashayams) boiled down continuously in heavy bronze and bell-metal vessels. This gentle extraction preserves heat-sensitive phyto-nutrients essential for nerve restoration.

3

Patient-Specific Potency Customisation

Because every individual's dosha imbalance and cartilage condition differs, our Vaidyar blends and warms the thailams specifically for each patient's daily session.

Patient Healthcare Advisory on Traditional Formulations

All herbal thailams, churnams, and kizhi boluses prepared at Navajeevanam are formulated for external application and traditional therapeutic support under the supervision of qualified Vaidyars. They are designed to relieve pain, support joint mobility, and improve localised circulation. Traditional therapies complement holistic care; prospective patients are encouraged to discuss any ongoing medical treatments and diagnostic imaging during their initial consultation.
